Capture degradation ladder decision: three options evaluated — <input type=file capture> (full resolution but no live preview, EXIF stripped on Android), getUserMedia+canvas (live preview, programmatic control, stamps performance.now() at shutter, but capped at ~1920x1080 on Android Chrome), ImageCapture.takePhoto() (spec says sensor-max but Chromium pipeline re-encodes a video frame when preview and capture are bound — takePhoto() often returns the same resolution as grabFrame()). Decision: getUserMedia+canvas as primary path, takePhoto() as best-effort enhancement. Probe getPhotoCapabilities().imageWidth/Height at runtime; never advertise full sensor resolution in the UI.
