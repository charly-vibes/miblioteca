ZUPT anchor design: pure IMU dead reckoning drifts ~1m in 10s on phone-grade sensors. The steadiness gate (|omega| > 0.5 rad/s or |a_lin| > 1.5 m/s²) enforces the device is stationary at every shot — this doubles as a zero-velocity update (ZUPT) anchor. Every CaptureRecord carries zupt: true. Downstream dead-reckoning chains ZUPTs to bound drift between photos. Without ZUPTs the motion trace is essentially unusable past ~1m. The ZUPT marker is free — it's an emergent property of the gate, not extra work.
